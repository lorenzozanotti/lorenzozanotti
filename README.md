### Projects

**Football analytics**

- [**The Pressure Game**](https://github.com/lorenzozanotti/DataViz-Project): Serie A 2024/25 beyond expected goals. PCA and K-Means reveal four tactical archetypes, with a case study on AC Milan.
- **Bayesian models for football counts** (BSc thesis): a flexible Bayesian regression framework in Stan for overdispersed, zero-inflated performance data.

**Machine learning & AI**

- [**AntibioticKG-RAG**](https://github.com/lorenzozanotti/Data_Semantics_project): an OWL/RDF knowledge graph from a citation network, comparing Graph RAG, Document RAG and a parametric LLM on multi-hop questions.
- [**Financial profiles & fraud risk**](https://github.com/lorenzozanotti/DS_Lab): Bank of Italy IACOFI 2023 survey data, SOM clustering, SHAP and a cost-sensitive fraud model.
- [**Credit card fraud detection**](https://github.com/lorenzozanotti/MachineLearning-Project): one million transactions with 8.7% fraud, four classifiers tuned for recall.

**Statistics & data engineering**

- [**Bitcoin market & news**](https://github.com/lorenzozanotti/DataMan-Project): Binance market data integrated with New York Times coverage and sentiment analysis.
- [**Italian elections 2022**](https://github.com/lorenzozanotti/ComputationalStatistic-Project): province-level clustering and socio-economic indicators.
- [**Rainfall in Hong Kong**](https://github.com/lorenzozanotti/DataMining-Project): predicting the probability of rain from historical weather data.
- [**Trending YouTube**](https://github.com/lorenzozanotti/FOCS-project): multi-country data processing and relational algebra in Pandas.
